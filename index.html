Small choices, better everyday living.

Use these as starting points, then check your school's official move-in guide and dorm policies before purchasing.

01 /

Bedding that works

Your bed takes up a lot of visual space, so keep it simple and comfortable.

  • Confirm Twin XL vs. standard Twin before ordering.
  • Choose breathable sheets and a washable duvet cover.
  • A mattress protector is a practical first purchase.
  • Keep colors simple so the room feels pulled together.
02 /

Make storage count

Shared rooms usually have less space than you expect. Measure before you buy.

  • Use under-bed bins for off-season clothing.
  • Keep daily items easy to reach.
  • Use a laundry bag that can travel to the laundry room.
  • Avoid bulky organizers until you see the room.
03 /

A focused desk

Make it easy to sit down and get work done without adding clutter.

  • Keep frequently used supplies in one organizer.
  • Use a comfortable desk light if permitted.
  • Manage charging cables with simple clips.
  • Keep the desktop mostly clear.
04 /

A room that feels like yours

Personal touches make a new place feel familiar. You don't need much.

  • Choose one or two colors to tie things together.
  • Bring a few photos or a small piece of art.
  • Coordinate with your roommate on shared spaces.
  • Check rules for candles, scents, lights, and wall hooks.

Prioritize before you purchase.

A starter guide to what matters most. These are categories and planning ranges, not paid endorsements or live product prices.

Must-have

Twin XL bedding

Sheets, pillowcases, and a comforter or duvet. Verify bed dimensions with your school.

Plan: $40–$100+
Must-have

Laundry setup

A hamper or laundry bag, detergent, and a few hangers make weekly routines easier.

Plan: $15–$35
Must-have

Shower essentials

Shower caddy, towel, shower shoes, and your regular toiletries.

Plan: $15–$40
Useful extra

Mattress topper

Can improve comfort, but check the school's mattress size and storage rules first.

Plan: $25–$70
Useful extra

Under-bed storage

Helpful for extra clothes and supplies. Measure clearance before choosing bins.

Plan: $12–$35
Can wait

Decor and extras

Art, extra lighting, organizers, and scent products can wait until you see the room and know the rules.
